Adaptation: HLSL guest-texture sampling, live anti-ringing uniform, premultiplied
RGBA and bounded alpha for transparent game artwork. Radial 4x4 weights and the
central 2x2 anti-ringing bounds follow the upstream shader. Window sinc 0.5,
sinc 1.0, default anti-ringing strength 0.8.
